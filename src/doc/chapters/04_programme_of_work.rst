Programme of work
=================

Deliverables
------------

* **D1 Record-side requirements.** One consolidated text of R1–R8, the lineage requirements, the absence states and the
  3 relations, each clause under its author's name. Requirements only: no encodings, schemas, protocols, identifiers,
  identity-binding mechanisms or conformance vectors.
* **D2 Cases.** A short mapping of the lineage cases LR1–LR5 and use cases #7 and #12 to the requirements they test.
  D2 fixes no conformance vectors.

Placement
---------

If the Focus Group places the work in an existing working group, or in one formed in Paris, D1 and D2 move into that
group's deliverables, and no competing charter is opened: "the requirements travel, and the drafting responsibility
travels with them" (@OlehVasylenko2000, 1 September 2026).

Editor
------

Anton Sokolov offers to serve as editor, as offered on FG-TIDA/themes#1 on 31 August 2026 ("willing to serve as editor
or co-editor, subject to the drafting timetable it adopts"), and asks for at least 1 co-editor for D1. Who co-edits,
and the cadence, are for the group to set.

Proposed timetable
------------------

Paris, 30 November – 1 December 2026: decision on the work item and its placement. Before the 2nd meeting (Geneva,
26–27 January 2027): a consolidated D1 draft for review. The editor attends in Paris on 30 November and can present the
item that day.

Attribution
-----------

Clause-level attribution stays traceable in D1, where every clause stands under its author's name, and in the linked
contribution record (see :ref:`chapter-annexes`): the theme 1 charter, which records who proposed and refined each
clause and keeps the open questions, and the 2 requirement comments on themes#1, which hold the clause text.
